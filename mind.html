Mind

Be better. Do better. Between the ears.

Riders and lifters already know: the session in your head decides the one on the dirt and the iron. Silent Actions keeps a free four-breath practice here. Kit is one tool. Breath is another. This is not therapy.

The four breaths

Ready

Inhale four · hold four · exhale four · rest four

Use it at the gate. Use it on the last set.

Box breathing is how you bring the body back online. Sit on the van bumper or the gym bench. Follow the orb. If it feels like too much, stop.

  • Gate

    Four cycles before the 30-second board. Jaw soft. Eyes on the first rut.

  • Floor

    One cycle between heavy sets. Then lift. Then one cycle when you rack.

  • Share

    1% of each order goes to community mental health. The kit funds the work beyond you.

Recovery

Ten minutes. Three doors.

01

Breathe

Four in, four held, four out, four still. Repeat four times. That is the whole ritual.

02

Move slow

Walk the pits. Stretch the hips. Water before the phone. Recovery is still the session.

03

Wear

Hood up. Street kit on. The point is to leave the track or the floor without taking the noise with you.

Silent actions for a better day

Small, repeatable, yours.

  • 01

    Water before the phone, before the first lap, before the first set.

  • 02

    Four breaths at the gate or on the bench.

  • 03

    One kind message after you train. Then put the phone in the bag.

  • 04

    Wash the kit the same night. Future you rides cleaner.

  • 05

    Name one feeling out loud. Then get on with it.
