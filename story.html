Our story

Named for the work nobody claps for.

The extra lap. The last set. The night you wash the kit and go to bed. Silent Actions is motocross, gym wear, and streetwear for people who train the body and still have to live in the head. The slogan is the whole brief. Be better. Do better.

Why three lanes

You do not live in one kit.

Saturday is the track. Monday is the floor. Tuesday is the street. We make all three so you are not buying a personality with every drop. Navy. Recycled where it takes a beating. Cotton where you want it on skin.

Certified

Fairtrade. B Corp. Social enterprise.

The kit is Fairtrade where cotton is used, the company is a Certified B Corporation, and Silent Actions is a Social Traders certified social enterprise.

Repair

Send it home. We stitch it back.

A blown knee or a dead zip should not bin the whole piece. We keep panels and thread for every run. Post it. We return it with the repair. More seasons on the body.
